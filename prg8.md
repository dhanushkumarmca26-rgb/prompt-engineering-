8. Problem Statement: Context-Aware Email Generation
A company wants to develop an AI assistant that automatically drafts professional
emails based on a user's situation and requirements. Design a prompt that generates
an appropriate email while following the specified tone, length, and content
constraints.
Input:
"Write an email to my manager requesting two days of leave due to a family function.
Keep it polite and professional, and limit it to 50 words."
Expected Output:
Subject: Leave Request
Dear Manager,
I would like to request two days of leave to attend a family function. Kindly approve my
request. I will ensure that my pending tasks are managed before my leave.
Thank you for your consideration.
Best regards
Constraints:
1. Maintain the requested tone and professionalism.
2. Do not invent personal details or reasons not provided by the user.
3. Follow the specified word limit.
4. Include only relevant information.
5. If essential details are missing, use placeholders instead of inventing them.
Objective: Develop a prompt that generates contextually appropriate emails while
adhering to user instructions and formatting requirements.
